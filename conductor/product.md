# Product Guide: Yolysi CRM

## Initial Concept
A specialized B2B CRM designed to automate the entire lifecycle of lead generation—from unqualified prospects to customer acquisition and loyalty—specifically targeting Shopify-enabled e-commerce companies.

## Product Vision
To build an intelligent, data-driven platform that ingests raw store data and transforms it into high-value customer relationships. By leveraging a mix of human, deterministic, and AI-powered workflows, Yolysi CRM identifies, scores, and nurtures leads to drive growth for B2B service providers in the Shopify ecosystem.

## Target Audience
- B2B companies and agencies providing services or products to Shopify merchants.
- Sales and marketing teams focused on e-commerce intelligence and outreach.

## Key Features
- **Data Ingestion & Synchronization:** Seamless data inflow via API or CSV imports from the Store Leads platform.
- **Advanced Enrichment Engine:**
  - **Store Verification:** Automatically detect if a site is active and still powered by Shopify.
  - **Entity Resolution:** Identify the operating company behind the storefront.
  - **Decision Maker Discovery:** Identify key people operating the company and e-store.
- **Lead Intelligence:**
  - **Scoring & Clustering:** Score leads based on multi-dimensional criteria and cluster them into Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs).
  - **Workflow Automation:** Hybrid workflows (AI + Human) to gather relevant information and refine data quality.
- **Lifecycle Management:**
  - **Tailored Nurturing:** Construct personalized nurturing paths for targets.
  - **Acquisition & Loyalty:** Tools to manage the transition from prospect to customer and foster long-term loyalty.
- **Enterprise-Grade Access Control:**
  - **Multi-User Architecture:** Support for diverse team roles.
  - **RBAC & Feature Gating:** Role-Based Access Control to manage permissions and gate specific features based on user roles or subscription tiers.

## Goals and Success Metrics
- **Data Accuracy:** High precision in identifying active, relevant Shopify stores and contacts.
- **Conversion Efficiency:** Streamlined workflows that reduce the time from lead ingestion to acquisition.
- **Scalability:** Robust architecture supporting high-volume data processing and multi-user collaboration.
